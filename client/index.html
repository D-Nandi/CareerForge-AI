Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to high-intent
career & ATS questions

What is an ATS-friendly resume?

An ATS-friendly resume is structured specifically so Applicant Tracking Systems can easily parse text, contact details, work history, skills, and education without layout corruption or unreadable graphics.

How does CareerNest make my resume ATS friendly?

CareerNest enforces standard section headings, clean typography, machine-readable PDF export, and real-time keyword scoring tailored to job descriptions.

What is a good ATS resume score?

A score of 80% or higher is considered strong. It means your resume matches primary role keywords, quantifiable achievements, and standard ATS formatting criteria.

Can AI write my resume and sound natural?

Yes. CareerNest uses Google Gemini AI to transform your raw inputs into professional, impact-driven bullet points with action verbs and metrics, avoiding generic buzzwords.

How do I match my resume to a job description?

Use the CareerNest ATS & Job Match tool: paste the job description alongside your resume to identify missing technical keywords, soft skills, and experience gaps.

What is the best resume format for freshers & students?

For students and freshers, a reverse-chronological format highlighting key coursework, academic projects, technical skills, and internship experience works best.

Is CareerNest completely free to build and export?

Yes. You can build, customize, and export professional ATS-ready PDF resumes with full styling control completely free without watermarks or forced paywalls.

How should I prepare for technical and HR interviews?

Use CareerNest's Interview Prep tool to practice role-specific coding concepts, system design scenarios, and behavioral questions using the STAR framework.
