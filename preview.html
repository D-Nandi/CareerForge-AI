Single-Page Resume Tips
Most recruiters spend <30s per resume — keep it tight & impactful!
  • ⚡
    Shorten bullets: Aim for 1-2 lines per bullet. Lead with strong action verbs and quantifiable metrics.
  • 🎯
    Reduce projects: Feature only 2-3 most relevant projects that highlight core competencies for target role.
  • 📝
    Compress summary: Keep professional summary concise to 2-3 sentences (50-80 words max).
  • 📏
    Reduce font size: Switch to Editor Canvas → Font Size → try 9.5px or 10px body font size.
  • 🛠
    Tighten skills: List only role-relevant technical and soft skills. Remove generic buzzwords.
  • 📐
    Use compact layout: The Minimal template offers the highest text density layout.
  • 💼
    2 pages is acceptable if you have 10+ years of experience or senior executive roles.
